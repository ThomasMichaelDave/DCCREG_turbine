How it is computed
The core is the solveDoubler4 topology (nodes 1–4; D1 2→ref, D2 3→ref, D3 1→3, D4 4→2) with continuous varicaps, run by the exact engine to its eigen-cycle. Energies scale as V², so every row is the same cycle scaled to its operating voltage (the highest node). Series: each 6-coil group carries its rail diode's conduction current (charge per cycle over the conduction time from the engine record); iron torque = window × N_c × ½ dL/dθ × ∫i²dt × f, PM torque = window × N_c × k_t × ī with k_t = N ΔΦ n_utron / 2. DC tap: a motor on the surplus, P = η × surplus (iron: η = the swing, PM: the field). Net T = motor − pump reaction (W × cycles per rev / 2π) − bearing − air shear (air only), stator standing. A pump-fed motor can never beat the pump's own reaction torque; only an outside source or a frame-reacted generator can counter-rotate the stator (sim/spinup-findings.md). Breakdown: air 24.4 d + 6.53 √d kV (d cm), vacuum 10 kV/mm design field [RH]. Defaults: sim/diode_machine.py CEM_DEFAULTS.
