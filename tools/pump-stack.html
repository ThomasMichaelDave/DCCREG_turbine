How to read the section
Vertical meridional cut through the axis, the same scale in r and z: the shaft is the vertical centre line, every horizontal stroke is one vane / plate seen edge-on (annulus r in … r out, so the full width is the plate diameter). Rotor vanes hang on the shaft sleeve, stator vanes on the outer posts; the fixed Ca / Cb plates are stator only (two nodes, alternating). Bottom = A side, top = B side, the bicone hub (C_R, AH coils, vacuum sphere) in the middle. Clocking: per station a rotor disc with its tip (red) under a stator ring with its sphere (amber), spheres rotated into the cut and labelled with their station angle. Reluctance: the designer's C-EM (core grey, spool green, coil amber) and utron, rotated into the cut. Right: a close-up.
Capacitance ladder
partvalueruletag
Tube C(θ) comes from a 2-D cell (stator vane | air gap | rotor vane | air gap, periodic in angle and along z), solved aligned and opposed at several radii and integrated over r. Inner and outer edge fringing is a fixed floor. "Strays fixed" keeps the disc design's absolute strays (20 pF per rail node …); "scaled" grows them with C_max. The real tube sits between the two until its strays are field-solved.
