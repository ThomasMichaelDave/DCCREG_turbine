Tube C(θ) comes from a 2-D cell (stator vane | air gap | rotor vane | air gap, periodic in angle and along z), solved aligned and opposed at several radii and integrated over r. Inner and outer edge fringing is a fixed floor. "Strays fixed" keeps the disc design's absolute strays (20 pF per rail node …); "scaled" grows them with C_max. The real tube sits between the two until its strays are field-solved.
